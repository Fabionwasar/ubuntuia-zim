## 📖 Word of the Day

**Word:** πνεῦμα *(Greek)*  
**Transliteration:** pneuma  
**Meaning:** The word 'pneuma' means 'spirit,' 'breath,' or 'wind' and is used in the New Testament to refer to the Holy Spirit, the divine presence and power of God among believers.  

**Scripture:** *John 3:8 - 'The wind blows wherever it pleases. You hear its sound, but you cannot tell where it comes from or where it is going. So it is with everyone born of the Spirit.'*  

**Reflection:** The concept of 'pneuma' invites us to consider the mysterious and transformative work of the Holy Spirit in our lives. Just as we cannot see the wind but can feel its effects, so the Holy Spirit moves in unseen ways, guiding, comforting, and empowering us to live according to God’s will.
