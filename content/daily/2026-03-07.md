## 📖 Word of the Day

**Word:** חֶסֶד *(Hebrew)*  
**Transliteration:** chesed  
**Meaning:** Chesed is one of the most profound words in the Hebrew Bible, encompassing steadfast love, lovingkindness, mercy, and covenant faithfulness. It describes the loyal, enduring love that God extends to His people — a love that is not earned but freely and abundantly given.  

**Scripture:** *Lamentations 3:22–23 — "The steadfast love (chesed) of the LORD never ceases; his mercies never come to an end; they are new every morning; great is your faithfulness."*  

**Reflection:** In a world that often makes love conditional, chesed reminds us that God's love for us is unshakeable and unconditional — rooted not in our performance but in His covenant faithfulness. Today, as you face your challenges and joys, rest in the assurance that His chesed surrounds you at every moment. Let this truth also inspire you to extend that same steadfast kindness to those around you, becoming a living expression of God's love in the world.
