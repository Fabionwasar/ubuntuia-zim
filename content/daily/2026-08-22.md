## 📖 Word of the Day

**Word:** πνεῦμα *(Greek)*  
**Transliteration:** pneuma  
**Meaning:** The word signifies spirit, wind, or breath and is often used to refer to the Holy Spirit in the New Testament.  

**Scripture:** *John 3:8 - "The wind blows where it wishes, and you hear its sound, but you do not know where it comes from or where it goes. So it is with everyone who is born of the Spirit."*  

**Reflection:** The term 'pneuma' encapsulates the mysterious and dynamic presence of the Holy Spirit. Just as the wind is unseen yet powerful, the Holy Spirit works in unseen ways to transform and guide us, inviting us to trust in His presence and leading in our spiritual journey.
