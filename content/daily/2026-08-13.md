## 📖 Word of the Day

**Word:** παράκλητος *(Greek)*  
**Transliteration:** parakletos  
**Meaning:** The term 'parakletos' refers to someone who is called to one’s side, especially in a legal context. In the New Testament, it is often used to describe the Holy Spirit as an advocate or helper.  

**Scripture:** *John 14:26: "But the Helper, the Holy Spirit, whom the Father will send in My name, He will teach you all things, and bring to your remembrance all that I said to you."*  

**Reflection:** In our spiritual journey, we are not left alone to figure things out. The Holy Spirit, our parakletos, walks beside us, offering comfort and guidance.
