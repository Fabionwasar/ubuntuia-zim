## 🎤 Short Sermon of the Week

### The Whisper of the Spirit

**Scripture:** *1 Kings 19:11-12, "The Lord said, 'Go out and stand on the mountain in the presence of the Lord, for the Lord is about to pass by.' Then a great and powerful wind tore the mountains apart and shattered the rocks before the Lord, but the Lord was not in the wind. After the wind there was an earthquake, but the Lord was not in the earthquake. After the earthquake came a fire, but the Lord was not in the fire. And after the fire came a gentle whisper."*

In our fast-paced world, filled with noise and chaos, we often long for a powerful, unmistakable sign from God. We desire the dramatic and the overwhelming to assure us of His presence. Yet, as we see in the story of Elijah, the Lord often chooses to reveal Himself through the quiet and the gentle. God does not always meet us in the whirlwind or the earthquake, but in the soft whisper. This passage reminds us that we must attune our ears and hearts to recognize the subtle ways the Holy Spirit moves among us.

Elijah, in his moment of despair and discouragement, found God not in the extraordinary phenomena but in the simplicity of a gentle whisper. The Ruach ha-Qodesh, the Holy Spirit, speaks to us in stillness, affirming that God is not far but intimately close, inviting us to quiet our souls and listen. When we allow the tumult of life to dominate our attention, we may overlook the profound peace that God offers. In the silence, the Spirit reassures us of His guidance and love, calling us to a deeper relationship.

As we reflect on this scripture, let us be intentional about creating space in our lives to listen for that whisper. Whether through prayer, meditation, or reflective reading of God's Word, may we seek the Holy Spirit's counsel in the calm moments we carve out amid our busy days. The Spirit's whisper guides us, comforts us, and empowers us to fulfill God's purpose. Let us not miss the gentle promptings that lead us to His wondrous plans, transforming our lives with His divine grace.

*— Rev Fab fire*
