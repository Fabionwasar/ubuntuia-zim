## 🎤 Short Sermon of the Week

### Empowered by the Spirit

**Scripture:** *Acts 1:8 - "But you will receive power when the Holy Spirit comes on you; and you will be my witnesses in Jerusalem, and in all Judea and Samaria, and to the ends of the earth."*

Beloved, our journey as followers of Christ is not one we embark upon alone. We are empowered by the Holy Spirit, the Ruach ha-Qodesh, who fills us with divine strength and guidance. Jesus, before His ascension, promised His disciples the gift of the Holy Spirit — a promise that holds true for us today. This promise is an invitation to live in the power and presence of the Spirit, who equips us to be witnesses of God's love and truth.

As we reflect on Acts 1:8, let us remember that the Holy Spirit's empowerment is not for our personal gain or glorification. It is to enable us to carry the message of grace and redemption to the furthest corners of the world. This mission begins in our own hearts and communities and extends outward, reflecting the ever-expanding reach of God's kingdom. The Spirit's power within us transforms our words and actions into testimonies of His goodness.

The Holy Spirit also provides us with a boldness that is not of this world. Where there is fear, He brings courage. Where there is uncertainty, He provides clarity. The early apostles, once timid and unsure, became pillars of faith and courage through the infilling of the Spirit. Today, the same Spirit empowers us to face challenges, speak truth in love, and bring hope to the hopeless.

Let us open our hearts to the Holy Spirit anew this week. May we seek His guidance and embrace His power, trusting that He will lead us in paths of righteousness for His name's sake. As the Spirit dwells within us, we become vessels of His light, called to illuminate the darkness around us. Remember, dear friends, we are not alone; we are empowered by the Spirit to fulfill the Great Commission, reaching out with the hands and heart of Jesus to a world in need.

*— Rev Fab fire*
