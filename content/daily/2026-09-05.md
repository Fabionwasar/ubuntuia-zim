## 📖 Word of the Day

**Word:** πνεῦμα *(Greek)*  
**Transliteration:** pneuma  
**Meaning:** The Greek word 'pneuma' is often translated as 'spirit' or 'breath,' referring to the immaterial, life-giving force from God, especially linked to the Holy Spirit.  

**Scripture:** *John 3:8: "The wind blows wherever it pleases. You hear its sound, but you cannot tell where it comes from or where it is going. So it is with everyone born of the Spirit."*  

**Reflection:** The concept of 'pneuma' embodies both mystery and power. Just as the wind is invisible yet perceptible, the Spirit's work in our lives might not always be visible but is undeniably transformative. Allow the Holy Spirit to move freely in your heart, guiding and sustaining your faith journey with His divine presence.
