## 📖 Word of the Day

**Word:** παράκλητος *(Greek)*  
**Transliteration:** paraklētos  
**Meaning:** Advocate, Helper, or Comforter — literally 'one called alongside to help.' Used in the New Testament to describe the Holy Spirit as the divine counsellor who stands beside believers, intercedes on their behalf, and empowers them for daily life.  

**Scripture:** *John 14:16 — "And I will ask the Father, and He will give you another Advocate to help you and be with you forever."*  

**Reflection:** The word paraklētos reminds us that we are never left to navigate life alone. The Holy Spirit is not a distant force or an abstract concept — He is a personal, present Helper who walks with us into every meeting, every trial, and every quiet moment of doubt. On this Monday, before the demands of the week press in, take a moment to acknowledge the Paraclete within you. Invite Him to lead, to counsel, and to comfort — and then step forward knowing that the most powerful Advocate in the universe is at your side.  

*— Rev Fab fire*
