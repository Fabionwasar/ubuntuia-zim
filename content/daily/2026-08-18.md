## 📖 Word of the Day

**Word:** πνεῦμα *(Greek)*  
**Transliteration:** pneuma  
**Meaning:** The Greek word 'pneuma' means 'spirit,' 'breath,' or 'wind.' It often refers to the Holy Spirit, the divine presence and power working in the believer's life.  

**Scripture:** *John 3:8, "The wind blows where it wishes, and you hear its sound, but you do not know where it comes from or where it goes. So it is with everyone who is born of the Spirit."*  

**Reflection:** Just as the wind is invisible yet its effects are evident, the Holy Spirit's presence in our lives may be unseen but is powerfully transformative.
