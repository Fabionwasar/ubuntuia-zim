**Word:** πνεῦμα *(Greek)*  
**Transliteration:** pneuma  
**Meaning:** In the New Testament, 'pneuma' refers to the Holy Spirit, the third person of the Trinity, embodying God's power and presence in the world.  

**Scripture:** *John 3:8, 'The wind blows wherever it pleases. You hear its sound, but you cannot tell where it comes from or where it is going. So it is with everyone born of the Spirit.'*  

**Reflection:** The Holy Spirit's work in our lives can be as mysterious and uncontrollable as the wind.
