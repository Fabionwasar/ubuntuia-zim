## 📖 Word of the Day

**Word:** πνεῦμα *(Greek)*  
**Transliteration:** pneuma  
**Meaning:** Spirit; often used in the New Testament to refer to the Holy Spirit, the third person of the Trinity, who is the divine presence of God in believers.  

**Scripture:** *John 14:26, "But the Helper, the Holy Spirit, whom the Father will send in My name, He will teach you all things, and bring to your remembrance all that I said to you."*  

**Reflection:** The Holy Spirit is our constant companion, guiding and teaching us in the ways of truth and righteousness. In moments of doubt or forgetfulness, the Spirit reminds us of Jesus’ words and promises, ensuring that we are never alone in our spiritual journey.

## 🎤 Short Sermon of the Week

### Led by the Spirit: A Journey of Transformation

**Scripture:** *Romans 8:14 - 'For those who are led by the Spirit of God are the children of God.'*

Beloved, as we gather this week in spirit and truth, let us ponder the profound reality of our identity as children of God. Romans 8:14 tells us that we are led by the Spirit of God, the Ruach ha-Qodesh. This divine leading is not just a reassuring promise, but a transformative journey that shapes us into the very image of Christ. The Spirit is our guide, our counselor, and our comforter, ensuring that we walk in the path of righteousness.

The Holy Spirit's work in our lives is not passive; it is an active force that empowers us to live out the calling we have received. In every decision, in every trial, the Spirit provides wisdom beyond our understanding and strength beyond our capabilities. When we yield to His guidance, we find ourselves being molded and refined, experiencing the fullness of God's love and purpose for us. As children of God, we are invited to trust and follow the Spirit's leading, even when the path seems uncertain.

Consider the transformation of the disciples at Pentecost. Filled with the Holy Spirit, their fear turned to boldness, their confusion to clarity, and their weakness to strength. This same Spirit dwells within us, equipping us to witness and work for the kingdom of God. Let us not be content with spiritual complacency, but let us seek the Spirit's fullness in every aspect of our lives. In our families, communities, and personal walk, may the Spirit lead us into deeper fellowship with our Heavenly Father.

As we move through this week, let us commit to being attentive to the Spirit's voice. Whether in the stillness of prayer or the busyness of daily life, may we be ever receptive to His prompts and nudges. Through the Spirit, we are empowered to live as His children, reflecting His love and grace to a world in need of hope. Trust in the leading of the Spirit, for it is through Him that we are transformed into the likeness of Christ, shining as lights in the darkness.

*— Rev Fab fire*
