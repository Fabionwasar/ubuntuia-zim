## 📖 Word of the Day

**Word:** πνεῦμα *(Greek)*  
**Transliteration:** pneuma  
**Meaning:** The Greek term 'pneuma' is commonly translated as 'spirit,' referring to breath, wind, or the immaterial aspect of a person, often associated with the Holy Spirit.  

**Scripture:** *John 3:8 - "The wind blows where it wishes, and you hear its sound, but you do not know where it comes from or where it goes. So it is with everyone who is born of the Spirit."*  

**Reflection:** The word 'pneuma' illustrates the mysterious and dynamic nature of the Holy Spirit, who moves freely and powerfully in the lives of believers. Just as the wind cannot be controlled or fully understood, so too the Spirit operates beyond human constraints, guiding and empowering us in our spiritual journey.
