## 📖 Word of the Day

**Word:** πνεῦμα *(Greek)*  
**Transliteration:** pneuma  
**Meaning:** The word 'pneuma' can refer to wind, breath, or spirit. In the New Testament, it is often used to describe the Holy Spirit, the divine presence that empowers and guides believers.  

**Scripture:** *John 3:8 - 'The wind blows where it wishes, and you hear its sound, but you do not know where it comes from or where it goes. So it is with everyone who is born of the Spirit.'*  

**Reflection:** The Holy Spirit moves in unseen and mysterious ways, bringing life and transformation to those who are open to its influence. Just as we cannot control the wind, we are invited to trust in the Spirit's work in our lives, allowing it to guide us toward deeper faith and understanding.
