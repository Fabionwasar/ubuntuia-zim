## 📖 Word of the Day

**Word:** חֶסֶד *(Hebrew)*  
**Transliteration:** chesed  
**Meaning:** Steadfast love, lovingkindness, or covenant mercy. Chesed is one of the richest words in the Hebrew Bible, describing God's loyal, unfailing love that flows from His covenant relationship with His people — a love that is not merely emotional but actively committed and enduring.  

**Scripture:** *Lamentations 3:22–23 — "Because of the LORD's great love (chesed) we are not consumed, for His compassions never fail. They are new every morning; great is Your faithfulness."*  

**Reflection:** Chesed is not a love that depends on our performance or worthiness — it is the love of a God who binds Himself to His people and refuses to let go. On the days when we feel we have failed, fallen short, or wandered far, chesed is the anchor that holds. This Tuesday, let the truth of God's steadfast lovingkindness be the lens through which you see yourself and others. Because you have received chesed without measure, you are empowered to extend that same loyal, patient, and generous love to the world around you.  

*— Rev Fab fire*
