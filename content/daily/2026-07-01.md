## 📖 Word of the Day

**Word:** פְּנֵמָה *(Hebrew)*  
**Transliteration:** ruach  
**Meaning:** Often translated as 'spirit', 'breath', or 'wind', ruach signifies the divine presence and life-giving force of God.  

**Scripture:** *Genesis 1:2 - 'Now the earth was formless and empty, darkness was over the surface of the deep, and the Spirit of God was hovering over the waters.'*  

**Reflection:** The presence of ruach in creation signifies that God is actively involved in shaping and giving life to the world. Just as the Spirit hovered over the waters, God’s ruach is present in our lives, ready to breathe new life and purpose into us if we open ourselves to His transformative power.
