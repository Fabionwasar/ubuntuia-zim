## 📖 Word of the Day

**Word:** πνεῦμα *(Greek)*  
**Transliteration:** pneuma  
**Meaning:** The Greek word 'πνεῦμα' refers to spirit, breath, or wind. In the New Testament, it is often used to denote the Holy Spirit, the divine presence and power of God active in the world.  

**Scripture:** *John 3:8: "The wind blows wherever it pleases. You hear its sound, but you cannot tell where it comes from or where it is going. So it is with everyone born of the Spirit."*  

**Reflection:** The work of the Holy Spirit in our lives can sometimes be as mysterious as the wind, unseen yet profoundly felt. As believers, we are called to live by the Spirit, allowing His presence to guide and transform us in ways beyond our understanding. Trust in His gentle, yet powerful influence, as He shapes us more into the likeness of Christ.
