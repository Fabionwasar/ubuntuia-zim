## 📖 Word of the Day

**Word:** πνεῦμα *(Greek)*  
**Transliteration:** pneuma  
**Meaning:** In the New Testament, 'pneuma' refers to the Holy Spirit, the third person of the Trinity, representing God's presence and power at work in the world and within believers.  

**Scripture:** *John 3:8: 'The wind blows wherever it pleases. You hear its sound, but you cannot tell where it comes from or where it is going. So it is with everyone born of the Spirit.'*  

**Reflection:** Just as the wind is invisible and unpredictable, so too is the movement and influence of the Holy Spirit in our lives. Allowing the Spirit to guide us requires faith and surrender, trusting that though we may not see the Spirit, its transformative power is always at work.
