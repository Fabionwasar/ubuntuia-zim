## 📖 Word of the Day

**Word:** πνεῦμα *(Greek)*  
**Transliteration:** pneuma  
**Meaning:** The word 'pneuma' primarily denotes 'spirit' or 'breath' and is used in the New Testament to refer to the Holy Spirit, the divine presence and power of God active in the world.  

**Scripture:** *John 3:8: "The wind blows where it wishes, and you hear its sound, but you do not know where it comes from or where it goes. So it is with everyone who is born of the Spirit."*  

**Reflection:** The Holy Spirit, like the wind, moves in mysterious and powerful ways, unseen yet profoundly impacting our lives.
