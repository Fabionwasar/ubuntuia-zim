## 📖 Word of the Day

**Word:** πνεῦμα *(Greek)*  
**Transliteration:** pneuma  
**Meaning:** The word 'pneuma' is often translated as 'spirit,' referring to the Holy Spirit, the third person of the Trinity, or the human spirit.  

**Scripture:** *John 3:8 - 'The wind blows where it wishes, and you hear its sound, but you do not know where it comes from or where it goes. So it is with everyone who is born of the Spirit.'*  

**Reflection:** The concept of 'pneuma' as spirit invites us to consider the mysterious and dynamic presence of the Holy Spirit in our lives. Just as we cannot predict the wind, we cannot fully understand the Spirit's work, yet it is powerful and transformative, guiding us in truth and renewing our hearts.
