## 📖 Word of the Day

**Word:** πνεῦμα *(Greek)*  
**Transliteration:** pneuma  
**Meaning:** In Greek, 'pneuma' primarily means spirit, breath, or wind and is often used in the New Testament to refer to the Holy Spirit.  

**Scripture:** *John 3:8 - 'The wind blows where it wishes, and you hear its sound, but you do not know where it comes from or where it goes. So it is with everyone who is born of the Spirit.'*  

**Reflection:** The concept of 'pneuma' as both wind and spirit beautifully illustrates the mysterious and unseen yet powerful presence of the Holy Spirit in our lives. Just as the wind is felt but not seen, the Holy Spirit moves within us, guiding, comforting, and empowering us to live out our faith in profound ways.  

*— Rev Fab fire*
