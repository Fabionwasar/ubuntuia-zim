## 📖 Word of the Day

**Word:** πνεῦμα *(Greek)*  
**Transliteration:** pneuma  
**Meaning:** The word 'pneuma' is often translated as 'spirit' and refers to the Holy Spirit or the spiritual aspect within a human, encompassing breath, wind, and life force.  

**Scripture:** *John 3:8, 'The wind blows where it wishes, and you hear its sound, but you do not know where it comes from or where it goes. So it is with everyone born of the Spirit.'*  

**Reflection:** The 'pneuma' reminds us of the mysterious and dynamic nature of the Holy Spirit. Just as the wind cannot be controlled or fully understood, the Spirit moves in ways we cannot predict or comprehend. Embrace the Spirit's guidance, allowing it to lead you into deeper faith and understanding.
