## 📖 Word of the Day

**Word:** πνεῦμα *(Greek)*  
**Transliteration:** pneuma  
**Meaning:** The Greek word 'pneuma' primarily means 'spirit' and can refer to breath, wind, or the immaterial part of a person. It is often used in the New Testament to denote the Holy Spirit.  

**Scripture:** *John 3:8, 'The wind blows where it wishes, and you hear its sound, but you do not know where it comes from or where it goes. So it is with everyone who is born of the Spirit.'*  

**Reflection:** The word 'pneuma' calls us to recognize the mysterious yet powerful presence of the Holy Spirit in our lives. Just as the wind moves invisibly but with force, so does the Spirit work within us, guiding and transforming our hearts in ways we may not fully understand. Embrace this divine presence by being open to its leading and letting it shape your spiritual journey.
