## 📖 Word of the Day

**Transliteration:** chesed  
**Meaning:** Steadfast love, lovingkindness, or covenant faithfulness. It describes God's loyal, enduring love that is rooted not in the worthiness of the recipient but in the faithfulness of His own character.  

**Scripture:** *Psalm 136:1 — "Give thanks to the LORD, for He is good; His chesed endures forever."*  

**Reflection:** Chesed reminds us that God's love is not a fleeting emotion but an unbreakable covenant commitment. No matter what we face today — uncertainty, failure, or fear — His steadfast love is the bedrock beneath our feet. Let this truth anchor your soul: you are held by a love that will never let you go.
