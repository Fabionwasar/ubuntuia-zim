**Word:** πνεῦμα *(Greek)*  
**Transliteration:** pneuma  
**Meaning:** The Greek word 'pneuma' is often translated as 'spirit' and is used to describe the Holy Spirit, the third Person of the Trinity, who is active in creation, revelation, and redemption.

**Reflection:** The Holy Spirit moves in unseen yet powerful ways, much like the wind. In our spiritual journey, aligning with the Spirit's guidance reveals God's presence and direction in our lives, encouraging us to live in faith and expectation of His work.
