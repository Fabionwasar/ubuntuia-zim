## 📖 Word of the Day

**Word:** πνεῦμα *(Greek)*  
**Transliteration:** pneuma  
**Meaning:** The word 'πνεῦμα' refers to 'spirit' or 'breath,' often used to denote the Holy Spirit in the New Testament.  

**Scripture:** *John 3:8, "The wind blows where it wishes, and you hear its sound, but you do not know where it comes from or where it goes. So it is with everyone born of the Spirit."*  

**Reflection:** The idea of 'pneuma' as both 'spirit' and 'wind' beautifully illustrates the mysterious and powerful work of the Holy Spirit in our lives. Just as the wind cannot be controlled or fully understood, so is the movement of God's Spirit, guiding us into deeper faith and transforming us from within.
