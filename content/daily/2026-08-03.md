## 📖 Word of the Day

**Word:** πνεῦμα *(Greek)*  
**Transliteration:** pneuma  
**Meaning:** The Greek word 'pneuma' refers to the Spirit, wind, or breath. It often signifies the Holy Spirit, representing God’s active presence and power in the world.  

**Scripture:** *John 3:8 - "The wind blows where it wishes, and you hear its sound, but you do not know where it comes from or where it goes. So it is with everyone born of the Spirit."*  

**Reflection:** In this verse, the mysterious and sovereign work of the Holy Spirit is likened to the wind, unseen yet profoundly moving. Embrace the Spirit's guidance, trusting in His unseen work, and be open to the transformative change He brings into your life.
