## 📖 Word of the Day

**Word:** πνεῦμα *(Greek)*  
**Transliteration:** pneuma  
**Meaning:** The word 'pneuma' means 'spirit', 'breath', or 'wind' and is often used in the New Testament to refer to the Holy Spirit.  

**Scripture:** *John 3:8: "The wind blows where it wishes, and you hear its sound, but you do not know where it comes from or where it goes. So it is with everyone who is born of the Spirit."*  

**Reflection:** The Holy Spirit, like the wind, is intangible yet powerfully present in the lives of believers. As we grow in faith, we are invited to respond to this divine presence, allowing it to guide and transform us in ways that are both mysterious and life-giving.
