**Word:** πνεῦμα *(Greek)*  
**Transliteration:** pneuma  
**Meaning:** The term 'pneuma' is commonly translated as 'spirit' and refers to the immaterial nature of God, often used to describe the Holy Spirit in the New Testament.  

**Scripture:** *John 3:8, "The wind blows wherever it pleases. You hear its sound, but you cannot tell where it comes from or where it is going. So it is with everyone born of the Spirit."*  

**Reflection:** The Greek word 'pneuma' highlights the mysterious and life-giving presence of the Holy Spirit. Just as the wind is unseen yet powerfully felt, so the Spirit moves in unseen ways to guide, comfort, and transform us. Embracing the work of the Spirit requires faith and openness to His gentle, yet compelling influence in our lives.
