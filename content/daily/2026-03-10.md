## 📖 Word of the Day

**Word:** δύναμις *(Greek)*  
**Transliteration:** dynamis  
**Meaning:** Power, might, or miraculous ability. In the New Testament, dynamis refers to the divine power of God at work in the world — the same power that raised Christ from the dead and now lives within every believer through the Holy Spirit.  

**Scripture:** *Acts 1:8 — "But you will receive power (dynamis) when the Holy Spirit comes on you; and you will be my witnesses in Jerusalem, and in all Judea and Samaria, and to the ends of the earth."*  

**Reflection:** The word dynamis is the root of our English word 'dynamite' — and that is precisely the kind of explosive, transformative power God has placed within you. You do not face today's challenges in your own strength; the same resurrection power that conquered death is available to you right now. Walk into this Tuesday with confidence, not because of who you are, but because of Whose power dwells within you.  

*— Rev Fab fire*
