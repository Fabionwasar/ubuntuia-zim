## 📖 Word of the Day

**Word:** πνεῦμα *(Greek)*  
**Transliteration:** pneuma  
**Meaning:** The word 'pneuma' often translates to 'spirit' or 'breath' and refers to the immaterial part of a person that connects with God, as well as the Holy Spirit, the third person of the Trinity.  

**Scripture:** *John 3:8 - 'The wind blows where it wishes, and you hear its sound, but you do not know where it comes from or where it goes. So it is with everyone who is born of the Spirit.'*  

**Reflection:** The concept of 'pneuma' invites us to imagine the unseen and powerful presence of God moving in and through our lives. Just as the wind cannot be fully controlled or understood, so too is the work of the Holy Spirit—mysterious yet essential to our spiritual rebirth and growth. Embrace the Spirit's presence today, trusting that even when invisible, God is dynamically at work within you.  

*— Rev Fab fire*

## 🎤 Short Sermon of the Week

### Walking by the Spirit: A Journey of Transformation

**Scripture:** *Galatians 5:16-17, "So I say, walk by the Spirit, and you will not gratify the desires of the flesh. For the flesh desires what is contrary to the Spirit, and the Spirit what is contrary to the flesh. They are in conflict with each other, so that you are not to do whatever you want."*

In our daily walk with Christ, the Apostle Paul provides us with a profound yet simple directive—'walk by the Spirit.' This call is an invitation to a life that is not only guided but transformed by the Holy Spirit. As believers, we are challenged to yield our hearts and minds to the Ruach ha-Qodesh, trusting that His guidance will lead us away from the snares of the flesh and toward the righteousness that God desires for us.

Our journey with the Spirit is marked by an intentional choice to seek God's presence and submit to His will. The flesh, with its worldly temptations and desires, constantly seeks to draw us away from our spiritual path. Yet, the Spirit within us is stronger, providing the power to resist and overcome. As we immerse ourselves in prayer, scripture, and worship, we open our hearts to the Spirit's transformative work, allowing Him to renew our minds and align our desires with God's perfect plan.

Walking by the Spirit is not merely about resisting sin but embracing the abundant life that God offers. The fruit produced in our lives—love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, and self-control—are the visible evidence of the Spirit's indwelling presence. Let us commit this week to actively listen and respond to the Spirit's promptings, trusting that as we do, our lives will reflect the beauty and grace of our Savior, Jesus Christ.

May each step we take be guided by the Spirit's wisdom and love. Let us remember that we are not alone; the Spirit is our constant companion, our advocate, and our source of strength. As we walk by the Spirit, may our lives become a testament to God's transformative power, drawing others to the hope and freedom found in Christ alone.

*— Rev Fab fire*
